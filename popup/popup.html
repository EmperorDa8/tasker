    Quick help
    How is time measured?

    Only the tab you are actively looking at is counted, and counting stops when you go idle or switch away.

    Where does my data go?

    It stays in this browser. Reports reach Google Drive only when you press Sync, and they go to your own Drive.

    How do I exclude a site?

    Open Settings and add it to the exclusion list. Excluded sites are never recorded at all.

    How is the work profile decided?

    From the tools you spend time in, on this device. It refuses to guess without several days of evidence, and you can override it in Settings.
