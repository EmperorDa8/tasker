❓ Quick Help & Tips
How does tracking work?

Tasker logs time spent on active browser tabs. It automatically pauses when you switch away or go idle.

Is my data private?

Yes, 100%! All logs stay locally in Chrome. Data is only sent to your personal Google Drive when you click Sync.

How do I exclude sensitive websites?

Click Settings at the bottom to exclude banking, email, or password manager sites with 1 click.
