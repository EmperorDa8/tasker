Privacy, by architecture

Local-first. No backend. Nothing to leak.

A time tracker sees your whole day, so we built Tasker to keep it to itself. Your history lives in chrome.storage.local on your device and stays there — no account, no login, no analytics. Sync to your own Google Drive when you choose. The AI monthly summary sends category totals only ("Development: 58 hours"), never a single URL, page title or note.

Your history stays putSites, page titles and times live in local browser storage and never leave it.
