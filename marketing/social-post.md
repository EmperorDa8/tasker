# Social post — Tasker

The device: the reader takes a five-second recall test in the first line and fails it. The
failure *is* the pitch. Everything after that is just showing them what they would have had
instead. Do not open with the product — opening with the product forfeits the whole effect.

**Pair it with the launch video** (`marketing/promo-video/out/tasker-launch.mp4`, 62s) or the
poster frame. Native video on LinkedIn and X materially outperforms a link post, and the
link in the body still carries the traffic.

---

## A — Flagship (LinkedIn, Facebook, Nairaland-friendly length)

**Before you scroll — what did you get done last Tuesday?**

Take the five seconds. Actually try it.

Most people manage one thing. Plenty get nothing at all.

That isn't a memory problem. Nobody kept the record. You spent eight hours across forty tabs
and none of it was written down — so by Friday it's gone, and by appraisal season, so is your
case for a raise.

I built Tasker for that gap.

It sits in Chrome and keeps the record while you work. Nothing to start, nothing to fill in.
At the end of the month it hands you something that reads like this:

*"You tracked 128 hours of focused work across 21 active days this month, with Development &
Code leading at 46% of your time. Research held steady in the second half, and your longest
uninterrupted streak ran 9 days."*
— example of a Tasker monthly recap

That took you four seconds to read. Reconstructing it from memory would take an hour and still
come out wrong.

Free. No sign-up, no account, no credit card. It's live on the Chrome Web Store — install it
and it starts working.

Your history stays on your machine. No account, no analytics, no server of mine holding it.

→ tasker-landing-liard.vercel.app

I built it solo and I'm still fixing it. Use it for a week, then tell me where it's wrong.

## Rules this copy follows

- **The recap paragraph is labelled as an example.** It is the sample data from the real
  product screenshots. Presenting it as a live user's real month would be a fabricated
  testimonial — label it every time it appears.
- **No install counts, no user numbers, no ratings** until they can be evidenced from the
  Chrome Web Store dashboard.
- **Authorship is disclosed** in every variant.
- **The privacy line stays whole.** "Stays on your machine" is accurate; if the post has room
  for only one caveat, keep it to what leaves the device rather than dropping the subject.

## Posting notes

- Lead with the video where the platform supports native upload. Put the link in the body on
  LinkedIn/Facebook, first comment on X if reach is throttled.
- Do not open with "Excited to share". It signals an ad before the reader reaches the hook,
  and the hook is the entire mechanism here.
- Reply to every comment in the first three hours. Comment volume is what carries reach on
  every one of these platforms.
