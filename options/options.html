Your daily logs and monthly accomplishment reports will be saved in this folder on your Google Drive.

🤖 AI Monthly Summaries

No API key or account needed. When you open a monthly recap, Tasker sends only totals — the month, your total tracked hours, how many days were active, and time per category. Your URLs, page titles, notes and milestones never leave this device. Turn this off and recaps are generated entirely offline. Fair-use limit: 3 AI summaries per month.

⚖️ What Counts as Productive

Your focus score is a weighted average of where your time went. The defaults suit a developer's week — if your job lives on LinkedIn, YouTube or Reddit, move those up so a good day reads like one. Changes apply to every day, past and present: the score is always recalculated, never stored.

⌨️ Keyboard Shortcut

Press Alt+Shift+L to record whatever you are looking at as an accomplishment, without opening anything. Chrome owns this binding, so it is changed on Chrome's own shortcuts page.

🔒 User Data & Privacy Notice

Tasker is privacy-first and local by default. All tracked activity durations, daily notes, and monthly recaps are stored strictly on your local browser using chrome.storage.local. Your browsing history never leaves this device. Only two things are ever sent out, both optional and controlled by you above: your own reports uploaded to your own Google Drive when you enable sync, and — if written monthly summaries are on — category totals only, with no URLs, page titles or notes attached.
