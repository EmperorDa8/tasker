Work profile

What the tools in your browsing resemble — and what Tasker refuses to guess.

Infer a work profile from my browsing Runs entirely on this device against activity already stored here. It is never sent anywhere — not to Tasker, not to the summary service, not into your Drive reports unless you sync one.
Reading your recent activity…
Your answer replaces the inference everywhere — the popup, the dashboard and the PDF, where it is labelled as stated by you rather than detected.

Tasker will report "not enough evidence yet" rather than guess. It needs at least 45 minutes on recognisable tools, more than one kind of tool, four distinct sites and three separate days — and when two profiles score too closely it shows the shortlist instead of picking one.

Job titles that describe the same work are reported as one profile with the other names listed beside it. AI Engineer, Applied AI Engineer, GenAI Engineer and LLM Engineer are the same tools in the same tabs, so browsing cannot separate them — and the pay difference between those titles is not something Tasker should pretend to detect.

Written monthly summaries

A short narrative at the top of each monthly recap.

Let Tasker write a summary paragraph No API key or account needed. Turn this off and recaps are generated entirely offline.

Only totals leave this device: the month, your tracked hours, how many days were active, and seconds per category. URLs, page titles, notes, milestones and your work profile are never sent. Fair-use limit: 3 written summaries per month.

What counts as productive

The focus score is a weighted average of where your time went.

The defaults suit a developer's week. If your job lives on LinkedIn, YouTube or Reddit, move those up so a good day reads like one. Changes apply to every day, past and present: the score is always recalculated, never stored.

Overrides the built-in guess for a site, subdomains included — so linkedin.com also covers business.linkedin.com.

Privacy & excluded sites

Sites matching these patterns are never recorded at all.

Comma separated. Matched anywhere in the URL, so bank covers every bank you use.

Even on sites you have not excluded, Tasker keeps page titles out of the record for banks, brokerages, mail, health and tax services — a title there tends to be the content itself. That list is a safety net; this exclusion list is the guarantee.
